Before you use Dravr sign-in

This extension lets you sign in to Garmin, Strava, TrainingPeaks or COROS yourself, in this browser, and hands that sign-in to Dravr so it can import your training.

What it reads

Only the cookies of the service you chose (garmin.com, strava.com, trainingpeaks.com or coros.com), and only after you click “Continue with” that service in Dravr and finish signing in on its page. It reads no other site and nothing you type.

Where it goes

Straight to Dravr (app.dravr.ai), over an encrypted connection, and nowhere else. Dravr stores the session encrypted and uses it only to fetch your data from that service. The extension keeps no copy.

You stay in control

Disconnect the service in Dravr to delete the stored session, and remove this extension to stop it.

Dravr privacy policy

All set. Return to Dravr and choose “Continue with” your service.
