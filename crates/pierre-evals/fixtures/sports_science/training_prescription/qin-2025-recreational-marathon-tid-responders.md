In 120 recreational marathoners training for 16 weeks, polarized training cut marathon time by 11.3 minutes versus 8.7 for pyramidal on average, yet training experience predicted the better model (r = 0.72): novices responded to pyramidal, experienced runners to polarized, and about 18 percent responded to neither.
