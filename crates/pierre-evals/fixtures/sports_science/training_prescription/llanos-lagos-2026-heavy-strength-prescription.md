The heavy strength programmes that improved cycling performance used about 4 lower-body exercises, 2 bilateral and 2 unilateral such as squat, single-leg press, hip flexion and single-leg calf raise, at progressive loads of 70 to 90 percent of one-repetition maximum (11RM to 4RM) for 3 to 4 sets with 2 to 3 minutes of rest, for at least 8 weeks at 2 sessions per week, with 1 session per week enough to maintain the gains.
