In 38 highly trained cyclists, 4 weeks of twice-weekly interval sessions, whether 8 × 60 percent of Tmax at the power eliciting VO2max or 12 × 30 seconds at 175 percent of peak power, improved 40-km time-trial performance by 4.4 to 5.8 percent, with VO2peak gains of 5 to 8 percent in the Tmax groups versus 1 percent in controls.
