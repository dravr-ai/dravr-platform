Across 51 studies and 1,261 athletes, high-intensity, sprint and repeated-sprint interval training all raised VO2max; gains from high-intensity intervals followed an inverted U peaking near 140 seconds of work at a work-to-rest ratio of about 0.85, and sprint intervals lost their effect when recovery exceeded about 97 seconds.
