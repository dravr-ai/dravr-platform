In 17 studies of 262 cyclists, heavy strength training, defined as lower-body lifts such as squats and leg presses at 80 percent or more of one-repetition maximum or 7 repetitions or fewer per set, done 1 to 3 times per week for 5 to 25 weeks, improved cycling efficiency (ES 0.35), anaerobic power (ES 0.56) and cycling performance (ES 0.46) without changing VO2max, with low certainty of evidence.
