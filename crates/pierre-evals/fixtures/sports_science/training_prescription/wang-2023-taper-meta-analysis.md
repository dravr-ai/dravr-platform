Across 14 studies, a progressive taper of up to 21 days cutting training volume by 41 to 60 percent improved time-trial performance (SMD −0.45) and time to exhaustion (SMD 1.28) without changing VO2max or economy, and an overload period before the taper enlarged the time-trial effect.
