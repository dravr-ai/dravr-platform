Norwegian world-class coaches across eight Olympic endurance sports all follow a traditional periodization with a gradual shift toward lower volume and more competition-specific training as the competitive period approaches, and combine a high volume of low-intensity training with two to three weekly key-workout days holding three to five intensive sessions.
