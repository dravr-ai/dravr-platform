In well-trained runners, 8 weeks of half-squats at 4 × 4RM three times per week raised 1RM by 33 percent and rate of force development by 26 percent, improved running economy by 5 percent and time to exhaustion at maximal aerobic speed by 21 percent, with no change in VO2max.
