In 33 trained triathletes, those functionally overreached after a 3-week overload gained less from the following taper than acutely fatigued athletes (supercompensation −2.6 percent), had a 70 percent infection rate versus 10 to 20 percent, and peaked mostly in taper weeks one and two.
