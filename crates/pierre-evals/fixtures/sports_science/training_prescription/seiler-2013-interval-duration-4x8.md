In trained cyclists doing two interval sessions per week for 7 weeks, 4 × 8 minutes at about 90 percent of peak heart rate produced overall gains of 11.4 percent in VO2peak, power at VO2peak and power at 4 mmol/L, versus 4.2 to 5.6 percent for 4 × 16 minutes, 4 × 4 minutes at about 94 percent of peak heart rate, or low-intensity training alone.
