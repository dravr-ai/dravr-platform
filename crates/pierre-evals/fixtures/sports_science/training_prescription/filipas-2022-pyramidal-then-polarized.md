In 60 well-trained runners training 16 weeks at constant load, a pyramidal phase followed by a polarized phase produced the largest gains (relative VO2peak about +3 percent, speed at 2 and 4 mmol/L about +1.7 and +1.5 percent, 5-km time trial about +1.5 percent), beating polarized-only, pyramidal-only and the reverse sequence, though every group improved.
