The Norwegian lactate-guided model prescribes three to four threshold-interval sessions per week paced by blood lactate of 2 to 4.5 mmol/L sampled every one to three repetitions, one VO2max session, and low-intensity volume up to 150 to 180 km per week, and depends on lactate measurement to hold the band.
