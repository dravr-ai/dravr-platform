Norwegian world-class endurance coaches build long intervals from 3 to 6 repetitions and short intervals from 15 to 25, keep threshold sessions controlled rather than to exhaustion, and about half prescribe double-threshold days twice a week at blood lactate of 2 to 4.5 mmol/L.
