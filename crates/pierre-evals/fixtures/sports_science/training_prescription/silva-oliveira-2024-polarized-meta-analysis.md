Across 17 studies (437 athletes), polarized training raised VO2peak more than other intensity distributions (SMD 0.24, high certainty), with the advantage confined to interventions shorter than 12 weeks (SMD 0.40) and highly trained athletes (SMD 0.46), and no difference on time trial (SMD −0.01), time to exhaustion or speed at the second threshold.
