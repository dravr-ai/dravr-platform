In 40 moderately trained men training three times per week for 8 weeks at matched total work, 4 × 4 minutes at 90 to 95 percent of maximal heart rate with 3-minute recoveries and 15/15-second intervals raised VO2max (7.2 and 5.5 percent), whereas lactate-threshold running and long slow distance did not.
