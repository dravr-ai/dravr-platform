Across 13 studies and 348 endurance athletes pooled at the individual level, polarized and pyramidal training did not differ in VO2max (SMD −0.06) or time-trial gains (SMD −0.05) overall, but competitive athletes gained more VO2max with polarized and recreational athletes with pyramidal distributions (subgroup SMD −0.63).
