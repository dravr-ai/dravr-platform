In 16 cyclists over 10 weeks, effort-matched 3 × 13 × 30/15-second intervals raised VO2max by 8.7 percent versus 2.6 percent for 4 × 5-minute intervals, with moderate-to-large gains in 30-second, 5-minute and 40-minute power.
