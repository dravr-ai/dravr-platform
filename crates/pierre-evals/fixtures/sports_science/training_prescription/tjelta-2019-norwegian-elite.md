Norwegian elite middle-distance runners structure two double-threshold days per week (e.g. 5×6 min @ ~LT2 in the morning plus 10×1 km @ ~LT1 in the afternoon) inside a high aerobic-volume base that uses lactate sampling to keep both sessions below 4.0 mmol/L.
