In general populations, endurance performance is maintained for up to 15 weeks on two sessions per week or a 33 to 66 percent cut in volume provided intensity is kept, and strength and muscle size, at least in younger people, are maintained for up to 32 weeks on one session per week of a single set at maintained intensity.
