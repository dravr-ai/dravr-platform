In 874 novice runners, those increasing weekly running distance by more than 30 percent had more distance-related injuries (patellofemoral pain, iliotibial band syndrome, medial tibial stress syndrome) than those progressing by less than 10 percent, while overall injury rates did not differ between progression groups.
