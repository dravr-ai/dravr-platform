Prescribing intensity as a fixed percentage of VO2max, maximal heart rate or maximal work rate has little merit for placing an athlete in a given exercise domain; LT1, the gas-exchange threshold and the ventilatory threshold validly separate the moderate and heavy domains, and only critical power or critical speed are well supported for the heavy-to-severe boundary.
