In well-trained cyclists over 12 weeks, block periodization with one week of five interval sessions every fourth week raised VO2max by 8.8 percent versus 3.7 percent for two evenly spread sessions per week, with a tendency toward a larger gain in power at 2 mmol/L (22 versus 10 percent).
