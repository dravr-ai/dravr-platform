Among the few trials comparing carbohydrate intakes of 100 g/h or more with 60 to 90 g/h during cycling, there is no clear evidence that the higher rate spares endogenous carbohydrate or improves single-day performance, while it may aid recovery across multi-day racing by raising total daily carbohydrate intake.
