Physiological resilience, the resistance of VO2max, economy and thresholds to deterioration during prolonged exercise, is promoted by years of consistent high training volume, regular prolonged sessions that include race-pace or harder bouts or a progressive rise in intensity under fatigue, and heavy strength and plyometric training.
